## 1. What these terms cover

When you register a provider key, Leadpoet may send lead order calls through
it. Those calls can carry information about people: names, job titles,
employers, work email addresses, LinkedIn profile URLs, and the requests and
responses around them. These terms call all of that "lead data". It includes
anything your provider shows you about those calls, such as logs, request
history and usage records.

## 2. Use lead data only to run lead orders

You let lead data pass through your key so Leadpoet can run lead orders.
You may not use it for anything else. In particular, you may not:

- contact anyone in it, by any channel;
- sell, share or publish it;
- add it to a list, database or CRM, or use it to enrich one;
- use it to train or evaluate a model;
- try to find out who a lead order was for.

## 3. Keep no copies

- Turn off request logging and data retention in your provider account
  wherever the provider lets you.
- Do not view, export or save lead data from your provider account.
- If you find a copy anyway, delete it within 7 days.
- Records your provider keeps that you cannot turn off are not your copy,
  but you must not open or export them.

## 4. Honour the suppression feed

The suppression feed lists people who opted out, as hashes. Never contact a
person on it, even through your own business. Use the feed only to honour it:
do not try to reverse the hashes or match them against other data.

## 5. Your key and your provider account

- The key and the provider account are yours, and your provider's terms apply

- Leadpoet spends on your key only within each order's spend limit.
- Protect the account with a strong password and two-factor sign-in.
- You can revoke your key at any time. Calls already started may finish.

## 6. Tell us about problems

Email hello@leadpoet.com:

- within 72 hours of learning that someone else may have reached lead data
  through your key or account;

  yourself; Leadpoet will.

are not wages and are not guaranteed.
